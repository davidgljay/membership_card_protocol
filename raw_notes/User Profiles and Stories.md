# User Profiles and Stories

_This raw document should be expanded through an interview._

## User Profiles

### Club Leader
A leader of a local Salsa club. He regularly uses WhatsApp and Squarespace, but is not otherwise technical. His club currently communicates on WhatsApp, and he is interested in expanding to use the membership card protocol to add additional resources.

### Club member
A member of the Salsa club. She is given a card by the club leader and will spend a small amount of time investing it before moving on. If she is intrigued she may check notifications from the club in the wallet app.

### Mutual Aid Leader
A member of a mutual aid network. They have numerous resources that they want to create, and are extremely concerned about digital security and privacy.

### Mutual Aid Network Participant
A participant in the mutual aid network. They are also concerned with security, and are interested in a tool that works seamlessly. They are concerned about default tech products, but find them familiar and are uncomfortable switching.

## User Stories

* A club leader wants to create a card and give it to a user via an open offer (sharing it via WhatsApp or Discord)
* A club member wants to accept a new card
* A club member wants to see what resources the card can be used to access as soon as they get their new card.
* A club leader wants to message members of the club via their cards.
* A club member wants to receive messages, and message other members to their cards.
* A club member wants to browse the multiple cards that they have received and see the messages that they have received.
* A club leader wants to create a new card policy, answering questions like “who can create this card?” And “who can revoke it?"
* A club member wants to access a resource that can be accessed with their card.
* A club member wants to log in with their card to a website that requests a card login.
* A club leader wants to review the cards that they have granted.
* A club leader wants to be able to revoke a card that they have the right to revoke.
* A club member wants to review a card that they have been offered through an open offer and decide whether to accept it.
* A club member wants to review a card that they have been offered via a message to another card and decide whether to accept it.
* A user receiving a card via an open offer wants to understand what it is, whether it is secure, and what they can do with it.
